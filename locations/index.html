Locations

One hospital, in Ishaka

KIUTH is on the Western Campus in Ishaka, Bushenyi District. A KIU notice describes that campus as about 300 kilometres from Kampala. The main campus in Kansanga is not the hospital.

KIU Teaching Hospital

KIU Western Campus, Ishaka, Bushenyi District, Uganda

The postal address printed on the hospital about page is the university’s main-campus box: P.O. Box 20000, Ggaba Road, Kansanga, Kampala. The hospital itself is in Ishaka, not at that Kampala address.

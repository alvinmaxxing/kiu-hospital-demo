Teaching hospital · Ishaka, Bushenyi

Specialised care for Greater Bushenyi

Opened in 2007 with 700 beds on the Western Campus. As of 6 October 2026 the hospital is in a stroke-data research project. A cancer, heart and neurosurgery expansion has been announced and is not reported open.

The directory searches names and titles published on kiu.ac.ug. Clinic days are not on that site.

Key services

Six areas with the strongest public descriptions. The full list marks what is still being developed.

Services A–Z

  • Accident & Emergency

    The hospital has an Accident & Emergency area. A dedicated emergency phone number is not published.

  • Maternity and gynaecology

    Antenatal, postnatal and gynaecology care are described in university sources. The department led the 2024 cervical cancer run.

  • Internal medicine

    Internal medicine is an academic department and a ward area included in the 2026 clinical tour.

  • Imaging and radiology

    Imaging was toured in August 2026. Stroke records through 2026 include CT findings.

  • Laboratory

    Laboratories support the wards. Reported tests include full blood count, C-reactive protein, and cultures.

  • Surgery

    General surgery is an academic department and was part of the 2024 national surgical camp led from this hospital.

Care, education and research

The hospital’s mission holds the three together: specialised care, training, and research in line with national health priorities.

Care

Emergency, maternity, medicine, imaging, laboratory and surgery are described in university sources. Several specialties are still being developed.

News

All news

  • 6 October 2026 · University news

    Hospital team trained for privacy-preserving health data, 28 September–2 October 2026

    The latest hospital report says site preparation for the stroke-data project is finished. Training ran through 2 October. The system is not described as open to patients.

  • 2 October 2026 · University news

    A $60 million cancer, heart and neurosurgery hospital is announced, not confirmed open

    KIU restated plans for a private cancer institute, heart institute and neurosurgery hospital at the Western Campus. The August 2026 clinical tour still described several of these services as being developed.

  • 22 September 2026 · University news

    Stroke-data research prepares to run at the hospital

    From 21 September 2026 the hospital hosted site preparation for a federated stroke-data project with the African Population and Health Research Centre.

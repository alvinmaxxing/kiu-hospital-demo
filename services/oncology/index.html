Oncology

Oncology is named as a service being developed. A separate cancer institute has been announced and is not confirmed open.

The August 2026 overview lists oncology among areas being developed. Cervical screening events are the only oncology-related service with a published community date.

On 8 January 2025 KIU said a private cancer, heart and brain-surgery hospital would launch in mid-2025. On 2 October 2026 the Vice Chancellor restated the plan as a $60 million expansion. Neither notice publishes an opening, a chemotherapy day or a radiotherapy service. This site does not treat that institute as open.

Source: KIU news, 17 August 2026, 8 January 2025 and 2 October 2026.

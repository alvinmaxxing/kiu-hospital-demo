Patients & visitors

Before you come to Ishaka

The hospital has not published a visitor handbook. The practical list below is general guidance. Items the hospital has not confirmed are marked.

Visiting hours

Placeholder · hours not published

Ward visiting hours, ICU rules and a maternity companion policy were not found on kiuth.kiu.ac.ug. Call +256 760 502660 and ask for the ward, or confirm at the entrance. Do not travel on an assumed visiting time.

What to bring

General guidance, not a hospital checklist.

  • A document with your name, and a referral letter if another facility sent you.
  • The names and doses of medicines you already take.
  • Any insurance card you hold. Cover is not guaranteed.
  • A way to pay, in case the service you need is not free.
  • A companion if you may be admitted. Ask the ward before extra visitors come.

Insurance

Placeholder · partners not published

No public panel of insurers, HMOs or government schemes was found. Bring your card and ask at registration whether that scheme is accepted for the service you need. Event sponsors of the 2024 cervical cancer run are not insurance partners.

Admission and records

The latest public description of hospital records is the 2026 stroke-data project, which uses records from 2015 to 2026 and keeps patient-level data at KIU. In 2021 the hospital said it had moved to a digital system called ClinicMaster. That system is not confirmed as the one in use now. Fees and a deposit amount are not published.

If it is an emergency

Go to Accident & Emergency on the Ishaka campus. A dedicated emergency number is not published.

Drawn from the hospital’s published values, not from a separate patient-charter document. A formal charter was not found.

  • Clear communication

    Information shared with the people who need it.

    Resources accounted for in a transparent way.

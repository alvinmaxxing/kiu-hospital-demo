News

From the public record

Stories are summarised from kiuth.kiu.ac.ug and kiu.ac.ug. Each item links to the university page it comes from.

  • 6 October 2026 · University news

    Hospital team trained for privacy-preserving health data, 28 September–2 October 2026

    The latest hospital report says site preparation for the stroke-data project is finished. Training ran through 2 October. The system is not described as open to patients.

  • 2 October 2026 · University news

    A $60 million cancer, heart and neurosurgery hospital is announced, not confirmed open

    KIU restated plans for a private cancer institute, heart institute and neurosurgery hospital at the Western Campus. The August 2026 clinical tour still described several of these services as being developed.

  • 22 September 2026 · University news

    Stroke-data research prepares to run at the hospital

    From 21 September 2026 the hospital hosted site preparation for a federated stroke-data project with the African Population and Health Research Centre.

  • 17 August 2026 · University news

    Uganda Heart Institute reviews cardiovascular care at KIUTH

    On 14 August 2026 the hospital hosted the Uganda Heart Institute to assess cardiology training, equipment and referral pathways.

  • 1 November 2025 · University news

    Africa Clinical Research Network inspects the hospital

    On 31 October 2025 ACRN toured the hospital laboratories, biomedical laboratories and the Institute of Biomedical Research to assess the site for clinical-trial collaboration.

  • 23 September 2024 · University news

    Free surgical camp, 21–27 September 2024

    KIUTH partnered with the Association of Surgeons of Uganda and the Ministry of Health for a week of free surgery in the western region.

  • 5 September 2024 · University news

    Visit to Kitagata General Hospital, 3 September 2024

    KIU reported a visit led by the vice chancellor. Sixty-seven medical students were training at Kitagata. One sentence in that story conflicts with the campus page on beds and the opening year.

  • 16 April 2024 · University news

    Cervical cancer run held, 13 April 2024

    KIU later reported that the run took place, with more than 200 people, screening, and blood donation.

  • 13 April 2024 · University news

    Cervical cancer run and free screening, 13 April 2024

    The Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology organised a run from the hospital, with free screening, HPV vaccination and treatment.

  • 5 March 2024 · University news

    Medical camp targeting more than 1,000 patients, 4–6 March 2024

    KIU reported a hospital camp for Greater Bushenyi from 4 to 6 March 2024. The published figure is a target of more than 1,000 patients, not a count of people seen.

  • 4 December 2023 · University news

    A diabetic clinic is named in a December 2023 hospital study

    KIU news described research among adults attending the diabetic clinic at the teaching hospital. A current clinic timetable is not published.
